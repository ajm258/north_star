# Reporting Specification

## Reporting philosophy

Reports are intentionally different by horizon.

- Daily = awareness
- Weekly = development
- Monthly = strategic portfolio management

They should not be three copies of the same report at different lengths.

---

# Daily report

## Objective

Prevent information blind spots.

## Sections

### 1. Portfolio snapshot
- current value
- daily P&L
- weekly/YTD context where useful

### 2. Significant gainers

For each notable gainer:
- ticker
- daily %
- relative volume
- sector/market movement
- brief apparent reason if known

A one-day gain should normally receive only brief explanation.

### 3. Significant decliners

Same structure as gainers.

### 4. Events
- earnings
- guidance
- filings
- major company news
- important macro/market events relevant to holdings

### 5. Early warnings
Fundamental or portfolio signals can appear even without a large price move.

### 6. Upcoming
Important events over the next several days.

### 7. Overall status
Example:
"2 items require attention; otherwise no material portfolio developments."

---

# Weekly report

## Objective

Understand what is developing.

## Sections

### 1. Portfolio performance
- weekly
- monthly/YTD context
- contributors/detractors

### 2. Persistent gainers
Criteria are deterministic and configurable.

Explain:
- cumulative move
- session persistence
- volume
- company/sector/market drivers
- fundamentals
- portfolio relevance
- reference levels

### 3. Persistent decliners
Same structure.

### 4. Fundamental developments
Positive and negative.

### 5. Thesis changes
- strengthening
- stable
- monitor
- potential deterioration

### 6. Price/fundamental divergence
Examples:
- price down while fundamentals improve
- price up while fundamentals deteriorate

### 7. Opportunities to investigate
Never phrase as an automatic Buy instruction.

### 8. Portfolio changes
- allocation drift
- concentration
- effect of transactions vs appreciation

### 9. Market wrap
Concise explanation of the week's major market movements and relevance to the portfolio.

### 10. Reference levels
Use neutral terminology:
- recent high/low
- historical reference areas
- moving averages
- valuation references

---

# Monthly report

## Objective

Strategic portfolio review.

## Sections

### 1. Executive summary
"Five things to know about the portfolio this month."

### 2. Performance
- monthly
- 3M
- 6M
- YTD
- 1Y
- benchmark comparison where available

### 3. P&L evolution
- realised
- unrealised
- total
- contribution vs new capital/withdrawals
- unrealised P&L trend

### 4. Portfolio structure
- allocation
- sector exposure
- concentration
- change versus previous month

### 5. Investment thesis review
All holdings receive a current thesis state.

### 6. Valuation review
Compare valuation with relevant historical/peer context.

### 7. Positive developments
Fundamental and portfolio opportunities.

### 8. Risks / investigation list
Material deterioration or concentration.

### 9. Market environment
Macro, rates, equity markets, currencies, commodities, major themes.

### 10. Changes since previous month
Highlight new or persistent developments.

### 11. Portfolio management summary
- positive developments
- monitor
- investigate
- potential accumulation candidates
- unchanged theses

No automatic Buy/Sell instruction.
